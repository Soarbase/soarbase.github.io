Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-23

This policy explains what Soarbase stores, where, and why. Soarbase is provided by Alexander Hjartarson, Iceland. Contact: alexanderhjartars09@gmail.com.

Without an account

If you choose "Continue on this device only", your flights, aircraft, photos and settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our database provider; we never see your password), and the pilot name, home airfield and licence number you choose to enter.
  • Flights: GPS tracks, flight statistics, notes and the IGC files you import or record, so you can use them on all your devices.
  • Friends and sharing: who you are friends with, and which flights you have shared. Flights are private unless you set them to Friends or Public.
  • Premium: your subscription status (active, renewal date). Payment details are handled by Paddle and never reach us.
  • Premium waitlist: if you leave your email on our website, we use it only to tell you when Premium launches.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), hosted in the EU.
  • Paddle.com (payments, as Merchant of Record), under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages (hosting this website and the phone version).
  • Map providers (OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, Esri, AWS terrain tiles, Google Fonts) receive standard web requests, such as your IP address, when maps and fonts load.

We don't sell your data and we don't use advertising trackers.

Location

Soarbase uses your device's location only while you are recording a flight, and only after you allow it.

Your rights

You can export your flights (IGC and backup), delete individual flights in the app, and ask us to access, correct or delete your account and data by emailing alexanderhjartars09@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Iceland: Persónuvernd).

Keeping data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your data, except where we must keep records by law (for example Paddle's payment records).
